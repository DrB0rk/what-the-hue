Choose the bulb

Use the six-character serial printed on the bulb, such as 92F796.

Enter the six hexadecimal characters exactly as printed.
This disconnects the selected bulb from its current Zigbee network. ZHA pauses during the reset and resumes automatically afterward. Other Zigbee devices and the network key are left alone.
